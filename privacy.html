1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, company, and project details when you fill out a contact form, subscribe to our newsletter, or apply for a role.

  • Contact details you submit through our forms

2. How we use it

We use the information we collect to respond to enquiries, deliver services you've requested, improve our website, and — where you've opted in — send occasional updates.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate our business (such as hosting or email delivery), under confidentiality obligations.

4. Data security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information in our care. No system is completely secure, and we encourage you to share sensitive information through appropriate channels.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us using the details below.
